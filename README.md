# News image: 22 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty
**[Sum up 22 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122)**.

## The entry

`entry.png` is a 1024×1024 newspaper front page, "THE WORLD TODAY", dated
**SUNDAY, 22 NOVEMBER 2026**. It was laid out in HTML/CSS and rendered with a
headless browser, so every headline is real rendered text — no AI-generated
imagery, no garbled type. The lead story is the collapse of the COP31 climate
talks; the other five stories follow in a clean two-column grid, each with a
short bold headline and one factual line. A small "Markets & More" strip adds
the day's market moves and two further items.

`main.ts` (Node, standard library only) copies `./entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`), byte for byte.

## The six stories

1. **COP31 CLIMATE TALKS COLLAPSE** — The UN climate summit in Antalya,
   Turkey, ends with no deal after the EU rejects a final text that omits
   fossil fuels.
2. **HORMUZ ON EDGE** — Iran says two supertankers struck naval mines east
   of the Strait of Hormuz; strikes on US bases in Bahrain hit a civilian
   facility, injuring 2.
3. **KRYVYI RIH HIT** — A Russian missile strikes Zelenskyy's hometown; a
   7-year-old boy and a 20-year-old woman are killed, about 60 wounded.
4. **MYANMAR GUILTY OF GENOCIDE** — The World Court rules Myanmar committed
   genocide against the Rohingya in 2017 — the first time it has found a
   state responsible.
5. **'EL CHAPO ISIDRO' CAPTURED** — Special forces capture the cartel boss
   in Sinaloa; 11 are killed in the raid, including 4 soldiers.
6. **DEMOCRATS WIN THE HOUSE** — Democrats take their largest US House
   majority since 1992 after a North Carolina recount.

Footer strip: S&P 500 −3.7%, Nasdaq −5.3% into correction; EU–Indonesia sign
a trade deal cutting tariffs on up to 98.5% of goods; a candy factory fire
in Cikande kills 3, 36 are missing.
